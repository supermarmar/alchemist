The preferential risk-weight tables for real estate exposure apply only where a bank's underwriting policy requires repayment capacity to be assessed independently of the property's expected value movement, and where the loan-to-value ratio, the loan amount divided by the property's value, is calculated on a conservative valuation basis. A national supervisor may withdraw the preferential treatment where a jurisdiction's real estate losses no longer justify it.
