A loan book's performance can be tracked along three axes, the origination vintage, the months on book, and the calendar month, which map onto a life table's cohort, attained age and calendar year respectively. Borrower age is a covariate on this reading rather than the actuarial twin of months on book, since two loans of the same age can sit at very different points on their own survival curves.
