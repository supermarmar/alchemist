A generalised linear model couples an exponential dispersion family response with a linear predictor through a smooth, strictly increasing link function, so the conditional mean is linear in the covariates once the link has been applied. The class covers a Bernoulli response through the logit link and a Poisson or gamma response through their own canonical links, among others.
