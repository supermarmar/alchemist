The metrics and diagnostics used to judge how well a binary classifier performs, including precision, recall and the F1 score derived from a confusion matrix, and the receiver operating characteristic curve traced out as the classification threshold varies. Different metrics suit different costs of a false positive against a false negative, so the choice of metric is itself part of the evaluation.
