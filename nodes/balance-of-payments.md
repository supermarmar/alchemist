## Definition

The balance of payments records a country's transactions with the rest of the world over a
period, and it balances by construction, since every transaction that gives rise to a
receipt or a payment is entered twice, once on each side of the accounts.

## The expression

$$
\mathrm{CA} + \mathrm{KA} + \mathrm{FA} = 0
$$

Here $\mathrm{CA}$ is the current account, covering trade in goods and services along with
income and transfers, $\mathrm{KA}$ is the capital account, covering capital transfers and
the acquisition of non-financial assets, and $\mathrm{FA}$ is the financial account,
covering cross-border investment and reserve flows. The identity holds subject only to
statistical error, entered as a residual balancing item.

## Why this node exists

A current account deficit cannot exist in isolation, since the identity forces it to be
matched by an offsetting surplus somewhere in the capital and financial accounts, most
often net inward investment. Exchange rate determination needs this balancing mechanism,
because it is the flow of capital financing a deficit that puts pressure on the exchange
rate in the first place.
