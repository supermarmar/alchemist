The canonical link of an exponential dispersion family member is the inverse of the mean function implied by its cumulant function, so it makes the canonical parameter itself linear in the covariates when used in a generalised linear model. Fitting under the canonical link guarantees a unique maximum likelihood estimator, where one exists, and delivers the balance property as an algebraic consequence of the score equations.
