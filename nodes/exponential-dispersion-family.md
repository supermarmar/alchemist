The exponential dispersion family is a class of distributions written through a canonical parameter, a cumulant function, a dispersion parameter and a weight, chosen so that maximum likelihood estimation of the mean coincides with minimising an associated deviance loss. The cumulant function determines the member of the family and, through its derivatives, the mean and variance that member implies.
