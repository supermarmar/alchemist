Regardless of the assessment method an entity otherwise uses, there is a rebuttable presumption that credit risk has increased significantly once contractual payments are more than thirty days past due. An entity can rebut that presumption with reasonable and supportable information showing credit risk has not in fact increased significantly, though the presumption does not apply once an entity has already identified a significant increase from earlier information.
