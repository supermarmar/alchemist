A financial asset or liability is initially measured at its fair value, adjusted by the transaction costs directly attributable to its acquisition or issue, unless it is measured at fair value through profit or loss, in which case those costs are expensed immediately. This adjusted amount becomes the gross carrying amount from which subsequent amortisation and impairment are calculated.
