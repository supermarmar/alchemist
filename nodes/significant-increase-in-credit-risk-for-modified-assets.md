Where a financial asset's contractual cash flows have been renegotiated without the asset being derecognised, an entity assesses a significant increase in credit risk by comparing the risk of default under the modified terms at the reporting date with the risk of default under the original terms at initial recognition. This keeps a lender from resetting a struggling borrower's credit risk clock simply by agreeing new terms.
