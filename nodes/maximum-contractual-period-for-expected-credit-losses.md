The period over which an entity measures expected credit losses is capped at the financial instrument's maximum contractual period, including any extension option, even where the entity's business practice is to hold instruments for longer. A longer period consistent with how the entity actually manages the book still cannot extend the measurement horizon beyond what the contract itself allows.
