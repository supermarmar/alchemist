Representation learning is the technique of letting an algorithm design its own features from raw covariates, producing a lower-dimensional mapping on which a simple predictor performs well, rather than requiring an analyst to engineer them by hand. Deep learning composes many non-linear layers to build that mapping, so later layers combine the structure earlier layers detect.
