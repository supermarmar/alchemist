A qualifying rating system must separately capture the risk of borrower default and the risk specific to a transaction, such as its seniority or collateral, except under the supervisory slotting approach, which combines the two, and a retail rating system organises exposures into pools sharing similar borrower and transaction risk instead of grading each facility individually.
