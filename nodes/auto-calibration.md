A regression function is auto-calibrated if the borrowers assigned any given prediction default at that rate on average, which asks every price cohort to be self-financing on its own rather than merely asking the whole portfolio to balance in aggregate. It is strictly stronger than the balance property, since averaging the auto-calibration condition over the portfolio recovers balance while balance says nothing about any individual cohort.
