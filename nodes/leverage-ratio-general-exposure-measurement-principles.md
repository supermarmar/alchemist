The exposure measure generally follows gross accounting values without netting for collateral, and a bank must apply to it the same deductions it applies to Tier 1 capital, exclude securitised exposure meeting defined derecognition criteria, and remain alert to any transaction or structure designed to reduce the measure temporarily around a reporting date.
