The adjustments needed to the four graduation tests when the data being tested contains duplicate policies, meaning more than one policy is held by the same life, since duplication reduces the effective number of independent lives below the raw policy count. Ignoring duplicate policies overstates the effective sample size and makes a test appear more conclusive than the data actually supports.
