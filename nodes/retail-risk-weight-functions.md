Each of the three retail sub-classes, residential mortgage, qualifying revolving retail and other retail, has its own risk-weight function built on the bank's own pool-level estimates of probability of default, loss given default and exposure at default, and none of the three carries the effective maturity adjustment that the corporate and bank functions include.
