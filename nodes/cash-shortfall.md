The present value of the gap between the cash flows contractually due to an entity and the cash flows it actually expects to receive, which is the basic building block from which an expected credit loss is calculated. A cash shortfall can arise even where an entity expects eventually to be paid in full, since the standard also treats a delay in payment as a loss, and the same idea applies to an undrawn loan commitment by comparing the cash flows due if it is drawn against those the entity expects to receive on that drawn balance.
