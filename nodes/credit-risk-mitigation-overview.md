Credit risk mitigation covers the techniques, collateral, guarantees, netting and credit derivatives, by which a bank reduces the capital it must hold against an exposure, subject to the conditions that no mitigated transaction attracts a higher charge than an unmitigated one, that the same protection is never recognised twice, and that the supporting documentation is legally robust.
