The proportional hazards model fitted by maximising a partial likelihood that depends only on the covariate effects, leaving the baseline hazard entirely unspecified. In the absence of tied event times the partial likelihood estimator has a known asymptotic distribution, which supports inference on the fitted covariate effects.
