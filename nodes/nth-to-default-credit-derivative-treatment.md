A bank that sells protection through a first-to-default or other nth-to-default basket must hold capital against the weighted sum of the referenced exposures up to the basket's notional, while a bank buying such protection may recognise it as credit risk mitigation only where the basket is built from credit default swaps or total return swaps referencing every underlying name.
