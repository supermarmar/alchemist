A financial asset is classified at amortised cost when the entity holds it within a business model whose objective is to collect its contractual cash flows and its cash flows pass the solely-payments-of-principal-and-interest test. This is the classification a bank uses for a loan it intends to hold to maturity rather than to trade or sell.
