Where credit protection covers only part of an exposure, a proportional, pari passu, split between bank and guarantor is capitalised pro rata on each side, while a tranched split, where the guarantor absorbs a specific loss layer, requires the securitisation framework's tranching rules; a materiality threshold below which the protection provider need not pay is treated as a retained first-loss position.
