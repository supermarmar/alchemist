A bank able to estimate probability of default for high-volatility commercial real estate uses the general corporate risk-weight formula, but with its own, higher asset correlation formula reflecting this asset class's greater loss-rate volatility, and falls back to supervisory loss given default and exposure at default where it cannot also estimate those.
