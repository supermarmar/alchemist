A purchased receivable is treated as retail or as corporate according to the nature of the underlying obligor, and a bank buying corporate receivables may use a top-down, pool-level default-risk estimate in place of grading each individual obligor, provided full or partial recourse to the seller does not by itself disqualify the pool from this treatment.
