Origination fees compensating an entity for evaluating and creating a loan, and commitment fees for a loan the entity expects to disburse, are treated as an adjustment to the effective interest rate rather than as immediate revenue. A fee for a service performed after the instrument exists, such as loan servicing, is instead accounted for under the separate standard on revenue from contracts with customers.
