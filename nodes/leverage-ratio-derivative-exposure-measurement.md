A derivative's leverage ratio exposure combines its replacement cost with an add-on for potential future exposure, calculated at netting-set level only where an eligible bilateral netting contract is in place, and collateral received against a derivative does not reduce this exposure even though cash variation margin meeting defined conditions may offset the replacement cost component.
