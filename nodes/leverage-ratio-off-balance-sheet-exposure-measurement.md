Off-balance sheet items are converted into a leverage ratio exposure using the same credit conversion factors as the standardised approach for credit risk, but with a floor of 10%, so that even an unconditionally cancellable commitment retains some leverage ratio exposure, and existing specific and general provisions cannot be netted against the resulting figure.
