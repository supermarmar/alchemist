For most asset classes a bank chooses between a foundation approach, under which it estimates only probability of default and uses supervisory values for the other risk components, and an advanced approach, under which it also estimates loss given default, exposure at default and effective maturity itself, subject to supervisory approval for each element it self-estimates.
