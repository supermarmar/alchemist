The main places a mortality forecast can go wrong, including model risk from choosing the wrong functional form, parameter risk from estimating that form on limited data, and the risk that a past trend simply does not continue into the future. Distinguishing these sources of error lets a forecast's uncertainty be communicated rather than left as a single unexplained margin.
