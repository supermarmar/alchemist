An off-balance sheet commitment or facility is converted into an on-balance-sheet-equivalent exposure by multiplying its notional amount by a credit conversion factor set by the type and cancellability of the facility, from 100% for a direct credit substitute down to 10% for an unconditionally cancellable commitment. The internal ratings-based approach's foundation approach applies the same factors.
