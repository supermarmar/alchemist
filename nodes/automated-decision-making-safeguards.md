A lender declining an application on credit reference information, or reaching a significant decision through automated processing alone, carries statutory duties to disclose the basis of the decision and to give the applicant a route to contest it, under provisions such as the Consumer Credit Act 1974 and the automated decision-making articles of the UK GDPR. Neither duty prescribes a ranked list of reasons, so the number and stability of any reason codes issued to satisfy it are a modelling and validation choice rather than a fixed compliance requirement.
