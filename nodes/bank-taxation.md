## Definition

Tax is the charge a bank bears on its profits, applied after all other costs, and it
reduces the return that ultimately reaches shareholders below what the pre-tax figures
suggest.

## The expression

$$
\mathrm{PAT} = \mathrm{PBT} \times (1 - \tau)
$$

Here $\mathrm{PBT}$ is profit before tax, $\tau$ is the effective tax rate the bank faces,
and $\mathrm{PAT}$ is the profit after tax that remains for dividends and retained
earnings. Bank-specific levies, such as a surcharge on banking profits, add to $\tau$ on top
of the standard corporate rate.

## Why this node exists

A return on equity calculated on pre-tax profit overstates what shareholders actually
receive, since $\tau$ takes a share of every unit of profit before it reaches them.
Consequently, every figure a bank reports as its return to shareholders is a post-tax one,
and comparing two banks on a pre-tax basis compares numbers neither of them ever pays out.
