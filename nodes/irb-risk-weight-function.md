The internal ratings-based risk-weight function for a corporate, sovereign or bank exposure converts probability of default and loss given default into a capital charge through a fixed formula built on asset correlation and the standard normal distribution, scaled by a maturity adjustment that rises with an exposure's effective maturity; risk-weighted assets then follow from multiplying that charge by 12.5 and the exposure at default.
