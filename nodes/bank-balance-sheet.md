## Definition

A bank balance sheet is the statement of its assets, liabilities and capital at a
point in time, constructed so that what it owns always equals what it owes plus
what it holds in capital.

## The expression

$$
\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}
$$

Here Assets is everything the bank owns, chiefly its loans, securities and cash,
Liabilities is everything it owes, chiefly deposits and wholesale funding, and
Equity is the capital cushion left over: the residual claim once liabilities are
met.

## Why this node exists

A bank's loans, deposits and capital instruments have to be recorded somewhere
before any of its lending or funding decisions can be analysed, and the balance
sheet is that record. Financial ratio analysis needs it next, since every
balance-sheet-based ratio is a comparison of two lines drawn from this same
statement.
