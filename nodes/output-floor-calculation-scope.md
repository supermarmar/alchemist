The 72.5% floor is calculated using a defined list of standardised approaches, the standardised approach for credit risk, the standardised approach for measuring counterparty credit risk, the standardised or basic approach for CVA, the securitisation framework's external-ratings-based or standardised approach, the standardised approach for market risk, and the standardised approach for operational risk, rather than a bank's modelled figures for any of these.
