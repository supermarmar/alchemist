Before assigning a standardised risk weight, a bank must carry out its own due diligence on a counterparty's credit risk profile rather than relying mechanically on an external rating, extending that check to entities within a consolidated group. Internal policies and controls must ensure the resulting risk weight is appropriate, and a material discrepancy against the rating-implied weight must reach senior management.
