Gradient descent minimises a loss by repeatedly stepping the parameter a small distance against the gradient of that loss, which is the workhorse optimisation method for a non-convex objective with no closed-form solution. The step size, or learning rate, controls how far each iteration moves and has to be chosen small enough for the iteration to converge.
