Discriminatory power measures how well a model ranks observations by risk, most commonly through the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve or its rescaling, the Gini coefficient. Because both statistics depend only on the ordering of the predictions, they are invariant to any strictly increasing transform of the fitted scores and so cannot detect a model whose level or spread is wrong.
