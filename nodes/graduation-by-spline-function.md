Graduation carried out by fitting a piecewise polynomial spline through the crude mortality data, with the degree of smoothing controlled separately from the choice of any particular parametric formula. This method is more flexible than a single parametric formula, at the cost of a smoothing choice that has to be made explicitly rather than following from a law of mortality.
