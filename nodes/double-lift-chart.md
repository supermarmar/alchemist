A double lift chart compares two models by binning on the ratio of their predictions rather than on either model's own score, so the bins are selected precisely where the two models disagree and the observed outcomes then adjudicate which model is closer in each bin. It answers a model comparison question a single lift chart on either model cannot, namely which model is right where the two disagree most.
