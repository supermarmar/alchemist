A corporate exposure is treated as specialised lending, rather than as an ordinary corporate exposure, where repayment depends primarily on the income generated by a specific financed asset rather than on the borrower's own independent capacity to repay. Project finance, object finance and commodities finance are the three specialised lending sub-categories the standardised approach recognises.
