The three broad approaches to forecasting future mortality rates: extrapolating past trends forward, building a model from an understood cause of improvement, and eliciting expert opinion about expected future mortality. Each approach carries its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice between them shapes how well the resulting forecast can be justified against past data.
