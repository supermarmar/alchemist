Under the comprehensive approach, a bank adjusts both the exposure amount and the collateral's value by supervisory or own-estimate haircuts reflecting the instrument's price volatility, any currency mismatch and, for a securities financing transaction, the frequency of remargining, before comparing the two to size the residual capital charge. The internal ratings-based approach uses the same haircut method, extended to a wider list of eligible collateral, to derive an adjusted loss given default rather than a substituted risk weight.
