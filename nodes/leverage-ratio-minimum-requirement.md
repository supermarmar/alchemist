## Definition

The leverage ratio minimum requirement is the floor Basel III sets under the leverage ratio
itself: a bank must hold Tier 1 capital equal to at least 3 per cent of its total exposure
measure at all times, regardless of how favourably its risk-weighted assets are assessed.

## The expression

$$
LR \ge 3\%
$$

Here $LR$ is the leverage ratio, Tier 1 capital divided by the total exposure measure, and
3 per cent is the minimum every bank must meet continuously rather than only at a reporting
date. A global systemically important bank faces a higher floor still, since a buffer is added
on top of this minimum in proportion to its systemic importance.

## Why this node exists

A minimum expressed only as a formula has no force until a number is attached to it, and this
is the number every bank's leverage position is tested against. The leverage ratio buffer for
systemically important banks needs this minimum next, since the buffer is defined as an
addition on top of the 3 per cent floor this node fixes, not as a freestanding requirement of
its own.
