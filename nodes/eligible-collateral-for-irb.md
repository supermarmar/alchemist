Beyond the collateral types recognised under the standardised approach, the internal ratings-based foundation approach also recognises eligible commercial and residential real estate and eligible financial receivables as collateral, each subject to its own legal, valuation and ongoing collateral-management standards before a bank may reduce loss given default for holding it.
