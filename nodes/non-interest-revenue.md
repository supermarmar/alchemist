## Definition

Non-interest revenue is the income a bank earns from its banking book operations that does not
arise from an interest margin, principally fees, commissions and other operating income.

## The expression

$$
\text{NIR} = F + C + O
$$

Here NIR is non-interest revenue, $F$ is fee income charged for services such as account
maintenance or payments, $C$ is commission income earned on activities such as arranging
insurance or investment products, and $O$ is other operating income that does not fit either
category. None of the three components depends on the spread between lending and funding
rates, which is what separates NIR from net interest income.

## Why this node exists

A bank whose income relies entirely on its lending margin has no cushion when that margin
compresses under competitive or rate pressure, so a second income line that moves independently
of interest rates changes how exposed the bank's earnings are to a single risk factor. Bank
income statement needs this node next, since it is where non-interest revenue is set alongside
net interest income to give the bank's total operating income.
