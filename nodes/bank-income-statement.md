## Definition

A bank income statement sets out its revenue, costs and profit over a period,
built up from its interest, fee and trading income lines net of costs, impairments
and tax.

## The expression

$$
\text{Net income} = \mathrm{NII} + \mathrm{NIR} + \mathrm{TI} - \text{Costs} - \text{Impairment} - \text{Tax}
$$

Here $\mathrm{NII}$ is net interest income, $\mathrm{NIR}$ is non-interest revenue,
$\mathrm{TI}$ is trading income, Costs is operating expenses, Impairment is the
credit loss charge for the period, and Tax is the tax charge. What remains is net
income for the period.

## Why this node exists

A bank's interest, fee and trading income lines mean little scattered across
separate ledgers, and the income statement is what draws them together into a
single figure for the period. Importance of the profit and loss account needs it
next, since it is this same bottom line that a bank's stakeholders read as the
summary of performance over the period.
