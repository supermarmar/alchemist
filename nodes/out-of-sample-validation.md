A model's in-sample loss on the sample used to fit it can be driven arbitrarily low by memorising noise, so a fair assessment evaluates the fitted model on a held-out test sample that took no part in fitting. The resulting out-of-sample loss, also called the generalisation loss, is the quantity model selection and model comparison should be judged against.
