The balance property holds where a fitted model's predictions sum to the observed responses on the sample used to fit it, which a canonical-link generalised linear model satisfies exactly as a consequence of its own score equations. Credit modellers call this calibration in the large, and it is an in-sample identity that carries no guarantee about a fresh sample.
