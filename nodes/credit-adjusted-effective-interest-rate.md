For a financial asset that was already credit-impaired when the entity purchased or originated it, interest revenue is calculated using a credit-adjusted effective interest rate, one that reflects the expected credit losses already priced into the asset, applied to the amortised cost rather than the gross carrying amount. This keeps the entity from recognising twice over a credit loss it already priced in when it acquired the asset.
