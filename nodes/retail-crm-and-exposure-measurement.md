A bank may reflect a guarantee or credit derivative's risk-mitigating effect on a retail exposure either through its pool-level probability of default and loss given default estimates or through the same substitution approach used for corporate and bank exposures, and it measures both on- and off-balance sheet retail exposure gross of specific provisions.
