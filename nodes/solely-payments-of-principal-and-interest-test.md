The test asks whether a financial asset's contractual cash flows represent solely repayment of principal and compensation for the time value of money, credit risk and basic lending costs, rather than cash flows carrying leveraged or equity-like risk. A financial asset must pass it to qualify for amortised cost or fair value through other comprehensive income treatment.
