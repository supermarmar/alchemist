A written credit derivative is included in the leverage ratio exposure measure at its full notional amount, reflecting the bank's maximum potential loss as protection seller, with a reduction permitted only where the bank has purchased an equivalent, offsetting credit derivative referencing an identical or sufficiently similar name.
