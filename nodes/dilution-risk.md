Dilution risk is the possibility that a purchased receivable's value is reduced, other than by the obligor's default, through a credit note, offset or dispute over the underlying goods or services, and a bank must hold capital against it in addition to the receivable's default risk unless it can demonstrate the risk is immaterial.
