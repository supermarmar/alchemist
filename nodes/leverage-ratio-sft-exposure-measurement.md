A securities financing transaction's leverage ratio exposure is generally its gross accounting value plus a measure of counterparty credit risk, with a bank acting as principal measured differently from a bank acting as agent, and an agent bank providing an indemnity or guarantee to a customer must include the resulting exposure only where it bears risk beyond the value of the collateral or securities involved.
