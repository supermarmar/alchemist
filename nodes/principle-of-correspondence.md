The rule that an individual can only contribute to the count of deaths at a given age if that same individual would also have contributed to the exposure at that age, so the numerator and denominator of an exposure based estimate are built on a consistent basis. Breaking this correspondence between the numerator and the denominator biases the resulting estimate of the transition intensity.
