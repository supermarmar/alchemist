The December 2017 package completing the Basel III reforms, addressing the excessive variability regulators found in banks' risk-weighted assets by tightening the standardised and internal ratings-based approaches to credit and operational risk and adding a leverage ratio and an output floor as backstops. It replaced reliance on internally modelled outputs alone with measures a supervisor can compare consistently across banks.
