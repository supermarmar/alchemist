Asset correlation, R, is the supervisory parameter in the internal ratings-based risk-weight function that represents how strongly two borrowers' latent asset values move together, calibrated by the Committee to fall as probability of default rises for most asset classes and adjusted downward for a small or medium-sized borrower or upward for exposure to a large or unregulated financial institution. The document states this correlation and the inverse normal function it feeds using its own notation, R and G, rather than naming the single-factor credit model each element derives from.
