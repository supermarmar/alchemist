Historical credit loss experience is a starting point for estimating expected credit losses, but an entity must adjust it for current observable conditions and its forecasts of future conditions, and strip out the effects of past circumstances that will not recur. An entity also reviews its methodology and assumptions regularly, checking that its estimates move in the same direction as the economic data, such as unemployment or property prices, that its own methodology says should drive them.
