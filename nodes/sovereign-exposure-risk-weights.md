The risk weight a bank applies to an exposure to a sovereign or its central bank, set by reference to the sovereign's external credit rating, or, at national discretion, at a lower weight for exposure denominated and funded in the sovereign's own currency. The same national discretion extends to a guarantee issued by that sovereign.
