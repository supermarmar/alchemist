An exposure to an individual or a small business qualifies for a reduced regulatory retail risk weight where it meets product, exposure-size and portfolio-granularity criteria, with a further reduction for a transactor who has repaid a revolving facility in full each month, and the full corporate or standard retail weight applying otherwise.
