A model in which an individual's hazard rate is the product of a baseline hazard common to everyone and a factor depending on that individual's covariates, so that the ratio of any two individuals' hazards stays constant over time. This structure lets the impact of covariates such as age, sex or occupation on the hazard be estimated without first specifying the baseline hazard's shape.
