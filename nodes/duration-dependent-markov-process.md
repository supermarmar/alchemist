A multi state model in which a transition intensity depends on how long the process has already spent in its current state, alongside its usual dependence on age or time, such as a sickness model where the recovery rate falls the longer a claimant has been sick. The Kolmogorov equations for such a process carry duration as an extra argument alongside age and time.
