When a financial asset's contractual cash flows are renegotiated without the modification triggering derecognition, an entity recalculates the asset's gross carrying amount as the present value of the revised cash flows discounted at the original effective interest rate, and recognises the difference from the prior carrying amount as a modification gain or loss in profit or loss. Any fees arising from the renegotiation adjust the new carrying amount and are amortised over the asset's remaining term.
