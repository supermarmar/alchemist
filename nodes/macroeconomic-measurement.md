## Definition

Gross domestic product measures the total value of the goods and services an
economy produces over a period, and how it is compiled shapes how every other
macroeconomic statistic derived from it should be read.

## The expression

$$
GDP = C + I + G + (X - M)
$$

Here $C$ is household consumption, $I$ is investment spending, $G$ is government
spending, $X$ is exports and $M$ is imports, so $X - M$ is net trade. This is the
expenditure approach: the same total can also be reached by summing income earned
or value added, and the three approaches are reconciled in the national accounts
rather than assumed to agree by construction.

## Why this node exists

A policy target stated in terms of growth, inflation or the balance of payments
means nothing until growth itself has a measured definition, and this node
supplies it. Aggregate demand and supply needs GDP next, since it is the
quantity whose level and growth rate that framework sets out to explain.
