Right-censoring arises whenever an absorbing event's date may fall beyond the edge of the observation window, so a loan or a life still open at the snapshot has not been shown to survive, only to have not yet failed. Life insurance and credit share this problem because both wait on an event that may not yet have happened, whereas general insurance's exposure is measured and complete once the period has run.
