The standardised approach groups CVA risk factors into six risk classes, interest rate, foreign exchange, counterparty credit spread, reference credit spread, equity and commodity, each with its own defined buckets and prescribed cross-bucket correlations, mirroring the structure of the standardised approach for market risk.
