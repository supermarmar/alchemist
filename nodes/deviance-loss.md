The deviance loss of an exponential dispersion family member compares the log-likelihood of an observation under its best possible parameter with its log-likelihood under an estimated mean, and it is zero exactly where the two coincide. It is strictly consistent for mean estimation, so minimising it recovers the same estimator as maximising the corresponding likelihood.
