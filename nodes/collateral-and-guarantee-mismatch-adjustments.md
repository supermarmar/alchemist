Where the maturity of a guarantee or collateral arrangement is shorter than that of the underlying exposure, the protection's recognised value is scaled down by the proportion of the exposure's remaining life it fails to cover, and a mismatch of under a year removes recognition entirely under most approaches; a currency mismatch between exposure and protection is permitted under every approach. The internal ratings-based approach applies the same adjustment.
