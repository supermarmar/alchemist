A foundation model reaches a downstream task through a ladder of adaptation methods running from cheapest to most invasive, from in-context learning and retrieval that change no weights at all, through parameter-efficient fine-tuning of a small added set, to full fine-tuning of everything. A bureau score is, in this reading, a lender's long-standing precedent for the bottom rung, since it is a prior fitted by somebody else on a population the lender consumes as a covariate rather than inspects.
