Once a financial asset that was not credit-impaired at origination becomes credit-impaired, an entity switches to calculating interest revenue on the amortised cost, the gross carrying amount net of its loss allowance, rather than on the gross carrying amount itself. Where the asset's credit risk later improves enough that it is no longer credit-impaired, the entity reverts to calculating interest on the gross carrying amount.
