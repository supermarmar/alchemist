A lift chart bins observations by their predicted value and plots the binned prediction and the binned actual against the bin labels on one ordinal axis, letting a reader read calibration as agreement within a bin, monotonicity as an increasing sequence of actuals, and discrimination as the gap between the lowest and highest bin. It is a standard model comparison device in general insurance rating as well as in credit scorecard validation.
