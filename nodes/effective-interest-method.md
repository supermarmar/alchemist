Interest revenue on a financial asset is calculated by applying the effective interest rate, the rate that exactly discounts its expected future cash flows back to its gross carrying amount, to that gross carrying amount. Fees, points and transaction costs that form an integral part of the rate are amortised over the asset's expected life rather than recognised immediately.
