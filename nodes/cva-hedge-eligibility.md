A hedge is eligible to reduce CVA capital only where it is entered into for the purpose of mitigating CVA risk and managed as such, and both the basic and standardised approaches restrict eligible instruments to those referencing the counterparty's own credit spread or, for an index hedge, a sufficiently representative basket.
