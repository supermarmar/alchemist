A mortality forecasting model that represents mortality rates across age and time using a penalised spline surface, with the penalty controlling how smoothly the surface can vary. It offers a more flexible alternative to the Lee-Carter and age period cohort models, at the cost of a smoothing parameter that has to be chosen.
