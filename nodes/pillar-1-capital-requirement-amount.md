## Definition

The Pillar 1 capital requirement amount is the currency figure a bank must hold once the
applicable minimum ratio is applied to its own risk-weighted assets. It is calculated separately
at the common equity tier one, tier one and total capital thresholds, since each carries its own
minimum percentage.

## The expression

$$
K = k \cdot \mathrm{RWA}
$$

Here $\mathrm{RWA}$ is the bank's total risk-weighted assets, $k$ is the applicable minimum
ratio, four and a half per cent for common equity tier one, six per cent for tier one, or eight
per cent for total capital, and $K$ is the resulting capital amount the bank must hold at that
threshold.

## Why this node exists

A supervisor cannot compare a bank's actual capital against a requirement expressed only as a
percentage, and a bank cannot plan a capital raise against one either, so the ratio has to become
a currency amount before either exercise is possible. Total capital requirement needs this node
next, since the bank's overall capital requirement sums this Pillar 1 amount with the buffers
and Pillar 2 add-ons layered on top of it.
