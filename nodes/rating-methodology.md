## Definition

Rating methodology is the process of turning assumptions about claim frequency and average
claim severity into the expected cost of claims that premium structure then loads up into an
office premium. The risk premium it produces is the pure cost of the risk transferred, before
any allowance for expenses, contingencies or profit is added.

## The expression

$$
L = f \times s
$$

Here $f$ is the expected claim frequency per policy, $s$ is the expected claim severity given a
claim occurs, and $L$ is the resulting risk premium, the expected cost of claims per policy that
premium structure's own $L$ term is built from.

## Why this node exists

A rate built from a risk premium alone still has to be turned into the price actually charged
and monitored once written, a separate exercise from estimating the risk premium itself. Rate
setting needs this node next, applying judgement and the wider premium structure to the risk
premium this node produces.
