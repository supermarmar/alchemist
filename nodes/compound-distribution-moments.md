The mean, variance and coefficient of skewness of a compound binomial, compound Poisson or compound negative binomial random variable, and the same three moments once a proportional or excess of loss reinsurance arrangement has been applied to the underlying losses. These moments are the usual entry point for approximating the aggregate claims distribution itself.
