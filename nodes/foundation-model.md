A foundation model is trained on a broad, diverse corpus so that its learned representations transfer to many downstream tasks with minimal task-specific change, a property called versatile adaptation that distinguishes it from a model merely pretrained on data resembling one task. Capability tends to follow a joint scaling law in compute, training data and parameter count, which is why later foundation models grow all three together rather than parameters alone.
