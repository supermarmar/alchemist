Within the corporate class, the internal ratings-based approach separately identifies five specialised lending sub-classes, project finance, object finance, commodities finance, income-producing real estate and high-volatility commercial real estate, because a bank often cannot estimate a reliable borrower-specific probability of default for this kind of asset-backed lending.
