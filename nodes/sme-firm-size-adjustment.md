A bank may reduce the asset correlation in the corporate risk-weight formula for an exposure to a small or medium-sized borrower, by an adjustment that falls as the borrower's reported sales rise, recognising that a smaller firm's default risk is less correlated with the systematic factor the formula otherwise assumes.
