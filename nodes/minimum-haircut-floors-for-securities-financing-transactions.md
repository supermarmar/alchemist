A non-centrally-cleared securities financing transaction that does not meet a minimum haircut floor, set by the collateral type and residual maturity, must instead be capitalised as an unsecured loan to the counterparty, a rule aimed at transactions that channel leverage to non-bank counterparties outside the prudential perimeter.
