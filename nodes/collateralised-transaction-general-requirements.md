A collateralised transaction is one where a bank's exposure is wholly or partly secured by collateral posted by the counterparty, and a bank must choose consistently between the simple approach, which substitutes the collateral issuer's risk weight, and the comprehensive approach, which adjusts the exposure amount by a haircut, applying legally robust documentation and sound collateral management throughout.
