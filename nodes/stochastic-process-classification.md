A stochastic process classified by whether it operates in continuous or discrete time and by whether its state space is continuous or discrete, with a mixed process combining one continuous and one discrete choice from that pair. Worked applications of mixed processes show how the same classification scheme covers models that pair a discrete state space with continuous time, or the reverse.
