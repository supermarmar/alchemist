## Definition

Production and cost theory links a firm's output to the inputs it employs
through a production function, then translates that relationship into the cost
curves the firm faces as one input is varied while the others are held fixed.

## The expression

$$
MC = \frac{w}{MP_L}
$$

Here $w$ is the wage rate paid per unit of labour, $MP_L$ is the marginal
product of labour, the extra output produced by one more unit of labour, and
$MC$ is the marginal cost, the extra cost of producing one more unit of output,
which rises once diminishing marginal product sets in.

## Why this node exists

A cost curve derived this way says nothing yet about whether unit costs fall as
the firm's whole scale of operation grows rather than as one input alone
varies, and economies of scale needs this node next to make that longer-run
comparison.
