The observation that a univariate time series model such as an ARMA process carries the Markov property once it is rewritten as a vector of several lagged terms, so that a multivariate Markov model with a suitably enlarged state carries the same information as the original univariate model. This rearrangement lets state space and Markov chain methods be applied to an ordinary time series.
