Under the standardised approach, the CVA capital requirement is the sum of separate delta and vega capital requirements, each calculated by netting the sensitivities of a bank's CVA against those of its eligible hedges before the aggregation step, and a supervisory multiplier scales up the resulting charge to compensate for model risk in the sensitivity estimates.
