For a facility that combines a drawn loan with an undrawn commitment the entity can contractually cancel at short notice, such as a credit card, the entity measures expected credit losses over the period it remains exposed to credit risk in practice, which can run beyond the facility's formal contractual notice period. This exception applies only where the contractual right to cancel does not in practice limit the entity's exposure to credit losses.
