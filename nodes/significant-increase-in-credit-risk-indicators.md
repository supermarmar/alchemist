A range of qualitative and quantitative signals can point to a significant increase in credit risk, among them a ratings downgrade, a widening credit spread, deteriorating operating performance, a fall in collateral value or a change in expected payment behaviour, considered together rather than through any single trigger. Where the available information is qualitative, an entity need not run it through a statistical model before it can support a stage move.
