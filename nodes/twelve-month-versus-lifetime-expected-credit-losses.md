The twelve-month expected credit loss is the portion of lifetime expected credit losses attributable to default events that are possible within the twelve months after the reporting date, each weighted by its own probability of occurring. This differs from a shorter-horizon estimate of the losses a portfolio of defaulting instruments will eventually suffer, and from the cash shortfalls expected within that year, though both are common misreadings the standard specifically distinguishes it from.
