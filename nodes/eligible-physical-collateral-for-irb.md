A supervisor may additionally recognise other physical collateral, and a general security agreement or floating charge over a wider pool of assets, as credit risk mitigation for internal-ratings-based purposes, provided a bank meets minimum requirements on the collateral's liquidity, an active resale market and a reliable, publicly available price.
