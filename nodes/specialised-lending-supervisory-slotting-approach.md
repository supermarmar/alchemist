Where a bank cannot estimate a reliable probability of default for a specialised lending exposure, it must instead map the exposure to one of five supervisory categories, strong, good, satisfactory, weak or default, each carrying its own fixed risk weight, with a preferential weight available for a strong or good exposure of shorter remaining maturity.
