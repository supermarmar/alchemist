The single implementation date, 1 January 2022, that the Committee set for the revised standardised and internal ratings-based approaches for credit risk, the CVA framework and the operational risk framework, alongside the phased introduction of the leverage ratio's G-SIB buffer and the output floor's calibration path running to 2027. From that date the 1.06 scaling factor previously applied to internal ratings-based risk-weighted assets under Basel II also falls away.
