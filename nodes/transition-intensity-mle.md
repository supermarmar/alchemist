The maximum likelihood estimators for the transition intensities of a Markov model, obtained from the likelihood built from an observational plan, together with their asymptotic joint distribution. In the case of a single decrement these estimators reduce to a Poisson approximation, since the number of transitions observed then behaves like a Poisson count against the total waiting time.
