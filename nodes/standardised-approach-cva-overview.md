The standardised approach for CVA adapts the standardised approach for market risk's sensitivities-based method to CVA risk, requiring a bank to calculate and report the sensitivity of its regulatory CVA to the market risk factors that drive it, subject to supervisory eligibility criteria and monthly reporting.
