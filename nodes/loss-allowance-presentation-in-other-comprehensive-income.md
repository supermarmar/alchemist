For a financial asset measured at fair value through other comprehensive income, the loss allowance is recognised in other comprehensive income rather than as a reduction to the asset's carrying amount on the balance sheet. The asset therefore continues to be presented at its full fair value even though a credit loss allowance has been recognised against it.
