Any panel indexed by three linked time axes, an entity's own age, the calendar period, and the cohort it originated from, can identify at most two of the three without an external constraint, because the third is always their sum. The problem is the reason a model carrying all three axes needs a constraint imposed by the modeller rather than estimated from the data.
