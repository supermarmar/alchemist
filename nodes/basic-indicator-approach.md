## Definition

The basic indicator approach sets a bank's operational risk capital requirement as
a fixed percentage of its average annual gross income over the previous three
years.

## The expression

$$
K_{\mathrm{BIA}} = \alpha \times \overline{\mathrm{GI}}
$$

Here $\overline{\mathrm{GI}}$ is the average of gross income over the previous
three years, counting only years in which gross income was positive, $\alpha$ is
the fixed percentage the Basel framework sets, 15 per cent, and $K_{\mathrm{BIA}}$
is the resulting capital requirement.

## Why this node exists

Operational risk needs a capital number before a supervisor can hold a bank to it,
and the basic indicator approach supplies the simplest one available, a single
percentage of income that needs no internal loss data at all. Basel III
standardised approach for operational risk needs it next, since it replaces this
one fixed percentage with a more risk-sensitive indicator built from the bank's own
business lines.
