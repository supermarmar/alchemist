The relationship between a model's bias, meaning its systematic error from being too simple, and its variance, meaning its sensitivity to the particular sample it was fitted on, and how both move as model complexity changes. Total prediction error is usually lowest at some intermediate complexity, where the fall in bias and the rise in variance balance each other.
