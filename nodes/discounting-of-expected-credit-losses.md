Expected credit losses are discounted back to the reporting date, using the effective interest rate determined at initial recognition, rather than being discounted to the date a default is expected to occur or left undiscounted. A purchased or originated credit-impaired asset uses its credit-adjusted effective interest rate for this purpose instead of the ordinary rate.
