Where an issue-specific external rating is available, a specialised lending exposure is risk-weighted from that rating; otherwise object and commodities finance receive a flat weight, and project finance receives a higher weight during the pre-operational phase than during the operational phase, with a further preferential weight for a high-quality project finance exposure meeting defined creditor-protection conditions.
