At each reporting date, an entity assesses whether a financial instrument's risk of default occurring over its expected life has increased significantly since the instrument was first recognised, comparing that risk at the two dates rather than comparing the resulting expected credit loss amounts directly. The assessment draws on reasonable and supportable information, including forward-looking economic forecasts, that is available without undue cost or effort, and a given change in that risk matters more where the instrument's risk of default was low to begin with.
