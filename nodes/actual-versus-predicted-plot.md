An actual versus predicted plot bins observations by quantiles of their predicted value and plots the empirical mean prediction against the empirical mean outcome within each bin, evaluated on a held-out sample so a flexible model is not flattered by its own fit. Where the model over-spreads its extreme cohorts relative to what those cohorts realise, or under-spreads them, the plot shows which.
