The residual classification for a financial asset that meets neither the amortised cost nor the fair value through other comprehensive income criteria, measured at fair value with all subsequent changes recognised in profit or loss. Derivatives and instruments held for trading typically fall here, since their cash flows or business model do not meet the more restrictive tests.
