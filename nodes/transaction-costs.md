Incremental costs, such as fees paid to agents, advisers and regulators, that would not have arisen had the entity not acquired, issued or disposed of the financial instrument. They adjust the instrument's initial carrying amount and, where they are integral to the effective interest rate, are amortised over its expected life rather than expensed at inception.
