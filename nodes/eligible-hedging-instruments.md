A derivative measured at fair value through profit or loss can normally serve as a hedging instrument, and so can a non-derivative instrument at fair value through profit or loss where the hedge is of foreign currency risk, but only a contract with a party external to the reporting group qualifies. These eligibility conditions determine which of an entity's financial instruments it may formally designate into a hedge accounting relationship.
