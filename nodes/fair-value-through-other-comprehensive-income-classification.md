A financial asset is classified at fair value through other comprehensive income when the entity's business model is achieved by both collecting contractual cash flows and selling the asset, and those cash flows pass the solely-payments-of-principal-and-interest test. Fair value movements on the asset are recognised in other comprehensive income rather than profit or loss, until the asset is sold or reclassified.
