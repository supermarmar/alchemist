The output floor phases in gradually from 50% of standardised risk-weighted assets on 1 January 2022 to its full 72.5% level on 1 January 2027, and during this period a national supervisor may cap the increase in a bank's total risk-weighted assets that the floor causes at 25% of its pre-floor figure.
