An exposure secured on residential property that a borrower occupies or rents out is risk-weighted on a sliding scale by loan-to-value band, either against the whole loan or, as an alternative national option, split between a low-LTV tranche at the lender's ordinary risk weight and the remainder at a higher weight; a materially cash-flow-dependent exposure instead uses a separate, higher-weighted table.
