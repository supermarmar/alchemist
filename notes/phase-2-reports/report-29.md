# Phase 2 batch 29 attachment report

40 nodes worked. 12 attached, 28 uncovered. 14 total attachments.

This batch runs from pension-fund-de-risking to prediction-interval alphabetically, and mixes
a bank's own staff pension scheme risk (ASSA F207/F107), the three Basel pillars, deep-learning
architecture (permutation invariance), statistics (Poisson family, point/interval estimation,
posterior derivation, prediction intervals), pure mathematics (group theory, polynomial rings,
power series, phase-plane analysis), general economics (perfect competition, Phillips curve),
life insurance risk categories (persistency, physical, policy data) and general investment
management (portfolio management, performance and risk attribution) against a credit-risk
research vault. Regulatory capital (the Basel pillars, SA-CCR, GLM/Poisson regression) and
credit-risk-adjacent deep learning hit real ground; the bank-staff-pension, pure-maths,
general-economics and general-investment-management clusters found almost nothing, as
expected of a vault built for credit risk research rather than those adjacent syllabi.

### permutation-test
Candidates: `methods/representativeness-c2st` (open, matches directly).
Attached. States the mechanism close to verbatim: "Inference is handled through permutation
testing: the observed AUC is compared against a null distribution generated by randomly
shuffling sample labels many times, giving a p-value for the hypothesis that the two samples
are exchangeable. This avoids parametric assumptions about the classifier's output
distribution."
