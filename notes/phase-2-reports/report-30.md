### proportional-reinsurance
Candidates: `concepts/reinsurance` (open, matches exactly, dedicated section), `methods/reinsurance-pricing` (open, covers non-proportional excess-of-loss premium principles, a different node's territory).
Attached: `concepts/reinsurance`. Its "Proportional and non-proportional forms" section states the node's own body almost verbatim: "Under proportional reinsurance the cedant and reinsurer share premiums and losses in a fixed ratio. Quota share, the simplest form, cedes an identical percentage of every policy... Surplus reinsurance instead lets the cedant retain the whole of each risk up to a fixed amount, its retention or 'line', ceding only the surplus above it."
