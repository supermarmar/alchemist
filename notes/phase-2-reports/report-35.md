**securitisation** (assa.f107.3.6-3, assa.f207.1.9-1, assa.f207.2.6-1, ifoa.sp5.2.1-6). Candidates: `regulation/bcbs-securitisation-framework` (attach), `regulation/uk-securitisation-regulation` (attach), `regulation/significant-risk-transfer-uk` (reject), `regulation/eba-sts-securitisation-criteria` (not opened, subordinate to the two attached). The BCBS article treats the pooling and tranching mechanism directly: "banking book securitisation exposures", deriving the tranche capital requirement from "the bank's own IRB capital estimates for the pool assets (the KIRB parameter), the tranche's attachment and detachment points, the pool's effective number of exposures". The UK regulation article adds the roles the node's "pooling... and selling claims" sentence implies: "originator, sponsor, original lender, securitisation special purpose entity (SSPE), and institutional investor", with the 5% risk-retention rule. Together the two cover the mechanism and the parties involved fully enough that no ledger remainder is opened. `regulation/significant-risk-transfer-uk` was read and rejected: it treats SRT financing practices, not the basic securitisation mechanism, and its one mention of "multi-layered special purpose vehicles" is a risk factor, not a definition.

**simple-approach-collateral-risk-weight-substitution** (bcbs.d424.sa.para-146). Candidates: `regulation/crr-credit-risk-provisions` (attach, partial), `regulation/eu-crr-2013-credit-risk` (rejected as the weaker duplicate). `crr-credit-risk-provisions` states the mechanism directly: "The simple method, available only under the standardised approach, substitutes the risk weight of the collateral for the risk weight of the exposure for the secured portion." Partial scope: the article does not give the 20% floor, the minimum life-of-exposure pledge, or the six-month remargining condition (Basel paragraph 147); ledgered as `bcbs-d424-sa-simple-approach-collateral-conditions`. `eu-crr-2013-credit-risk` states the same mechanism in one sentence with no more detail, so the dedicated article was kept and the overview left off per the brief's rule on two articles covering the same ground.
