### initial-and-variation-margin
Candidates: `regulation/counterparty-credit-risk-saccr` (open, matches with a caveat), `regulation/bcbs-sa-ccr-standard` (open, rejected), `regulation/margin-streamlining-vm-non-centrally-cleared` (open, rejected), `regulation/uk-margin-requirements-nccd` (open, rejected).
Attached: `regulation/counterparty-credit-risk-saccr`. Its margining paragraph states both margin types' risk-mitigation function directly: initial margin is "calibrated for adequacy" and "respond[s] to changes in counterparty risk profile, portfolio directionality, concentration, and leverage" (the future-exposure function), and "variation margin should be applied daily" (the settle-the-change function). Partial-scope limit: the article never states the textbook "potential future exposure" / "settles the change since the last call" framing verbatim, so the section stays ledgered as a remainder. Rejected `bcbs-sa-ccr-standard`: it defines PFE and replacement cost precisely but as EAD capital-charge components, and never once uses the term "initial margin". Rejected `margin-streamlining-vm-non-centrally-cleared` and `uk-margin-requirements-nccd`: both are reform-history and consultation articles that assume the reader already knows what IM and VM are rather than defining them.

### insurance-regulation
