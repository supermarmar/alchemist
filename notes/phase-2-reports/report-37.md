## Attached

**structural-credit-model.** Considered: `methods/structural-credit-risk-models` (attach). The article is a dedicated treatment of Merton's 1973 construction: "A structural model derives default from the firm's balance sheet rather than fitting it to observed default rates," with equity as a call option on firm assets and risky debt as the residual claim. This matches the node's body precisely, though the article treats only Merton by name and never KMV.

**survival-function.** Considered: `methods/default-correlation-copula-models` (attach), `methods/lgd-survival-analysis` (rejected), `methods/ifrs9-lgd-term-structure` (rejected). The copula article gives the exact definition: "Li defines the time-until-default of a security as a continuous random variable measuring the length of time from today until default occurs, with the survival function giving the probability that this time exceeds a given horizon," and explicitly notes the actuarial framing the node's own body draws on. The two LGD articles use a survival function operationally without defining it, so they were not attached alongside it.

**survival-model-credit-risk.** Considered: `methods/survival-analysis-macroeconomic-pd` (attach), `methods/corporate-default-prediction-models` (rejected as the broader overview). Bellotti and Crook's Cox proportional-hazards model is the node's subject directly: "a Cox model with time-varying covariates lets each macroeconomic variable enter at the value it takes at the moment of default or censoring," modelling time to default rather than a fixed-horizon binary outcome. The corporate-default article covers the same historical arc (Altman to hazard models) more broadly and was left off per the dedicated-over-overview rule.

**systemic-risk-buffer.** Considered: `regulation/pra-capital-buffers-ccyb-srb` (attach). PS8/25 names the SRB as one of five UK capital-buffer instruments and sets out its implementation and identification methodology under the post-FSMA 2023 framework.

**tabpfn** and **tabular-foundation-model.** Considered: `concepts/in-context-learning-tabular-actuarial-models` (attach both), `methods/pragma-foundation-model` and `concepts/foundation-models-credit-risk` (rejected, wrong sub-topic: sequential event-stream foundation models, not tabular). The article's "Tabular foundation models" section states TabPFN "approximates the Bayesian posterior given its context by alternating attention across columns and across rows, handling a whole table in a single forward pass," and separately that both TabPFN and TabICL "are trained on synthetic tasks drawn from a prior, with no real portfolio in their training data," covering both nodes directly from the same section.

**stressed-outflow-report** (partial). Considered: `regulation/bcbs-liquidity-risk-principles-evolution` (attach, partial). The F207 treatment of sustainable bank strategy and governance itself is not present and is ledgered.
